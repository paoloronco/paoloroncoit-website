## A Journey Through My Experience

My portfolio website is designed to give you a detailed look at my experience and the most significant projects I have completed over the years. When you enter the site, you will be greeted by a soothing blue background adorned with interactive bubbles that animate as your mouse passes over them. These bubbles are more than just a visual attraction: they represent the various areas in which I have developed expertise and worked on projects.

## Discover the Software, Apps, and Implementations I Have Built

Each bubble on the site represents a specific piece of software, application, or implementation that I have developed or used in the past. Click on one of the bubbles and you will enter a world of detailed information about the selected topic. From programming languages and frameworks to development tools and completed projects, you will find a comprehensive overview of everything I have done and used over the years.
